# Unseen-video confidence calibration, preregistered protocol

- Freeze answer policy: Qwen3.5-2B + `runs/jev10_action_sft_v1/checkpoints/step_4813`.
- Source: `/pfs/qcy/JeV-Data/video_multiple_choice_clean_48126.json`, read only. Write all derived files below `/pfs/hyx/videojev-rlcd`.
- Exclude every question ID, video path and video filename in `data/jev10_v2/{train,validation,test}.jsonl` and the three prior blind sets (`jev10_blind_v1`, `jev10_reward_blind_v1`, `jev10_schedule_blind_v1`). Group remaining questions by video filename. Seed 20261005. Freeze 1,000 final-test, 500 development and 4,000 fit questions in that order, with no video overlap. Do not inspect final-test outcomes before fitting and selecting models.
- Produce greedy answer actions and action-token softmax probabilities with the frozen answer policy once; cache labels and distributions. Do not change the answer policy for confidence training.
- Fit a two-parameter Platt sigmoid on logit of chosen-action probability on the 4,000 fit examples. Fit a small head using frozen action-distribution features, including chosen-action log probability, on the same fit examples. Select head epoch by Brier on the 500 development examples.
- Train a separate confidence LoRA initialized from the answer adapter on the 4,000 fit examples. Use the cached greedy action and correctness as labels; minimize `(E[q]-y)^2`, with `E[q]` over the 10 confidence token probabilities. Use gradient accumulation of 8 examples per optimizer update, and save after each 1,000 examples. Select its checkpoint by development Brier. No answer-model gradient or confidence sampling.
- Evaluate the frozen original confidence, Platt, small head and development-selected confidence LoRA on the same 1,000 final-test questions once. Report accuracy, mean confidence, Brier, and constant baseline `p_test*(1-p_test)` on every split. Also report the fit-rate constant Brier to distinguish deployable constant prediction from the test-set oracle constant. Promote a learned confidence method only if its final-test Brier beats Platt.
- Selection and promotion use continuous mean confidence; rounded displayed percent is secondary.
